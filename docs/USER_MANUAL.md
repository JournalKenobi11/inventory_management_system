## 1. Introduction

### What is This Application?
The **Garage & Inventory Management System** is a standalone, all-in-one software tool designed to manage the daily operations of an automotive repair workshop, motorcycle garage, or spare parts store.

### Who Should Use It?
- **Garage Owners & Workshop Proprietors:** To monitor shop earnings, control stock, oversee payroll, track operating expenses, and protect business records.
- **Service Advisors & Mechanics:** To register incoming customer vehicles, create accurate bills, add labour charges, and record replacement parts used during repairs.
- **Inventory Managers:** To keep an exact count of spare parts on shelves, receive incoming stock deliveries from suppliers, and prevent shortages of critical components.

### What Business Activities Does It Help Manage?
- **Customer CRM:** Keep customer contact numbers, vehicle models, and full repair histories at your fingertips.
- **Stock Management:** Track live stock levels, cost prices, selling prices, and receive alerts when parts are running low.
- **Fast Job-Card Billing:** Generate clean, itemized customer bills with parts and labour charges, with automatic stock deduction and invoice numbering.
- **Digital Invoicing:** Download invoices as professional PDF documents and share them directly with customers via WhatsApp.
- **Business Expense Tracking:** Record daily workshop costs like shop rent, electricity, tea, tools, and consumables.
- **Owner's Personal Money Ledger:** Keep your private pocket money, withdrawals, and personal expenses strictly separate from shop finances.
- **Staff Payroll & Attendance:** Track employee monthly compensation, record salary advances or final payments, and track daily clock-in punctuality.
- **Executive Reports & Analytics:** Instantly view daily, monthly, or yearly profits, margins, top customers, and best-selling parts.
- **Data Protection:** Create regular backup files and export full database archives that you can save or share via WhatsApp or Google Drive.

### 100% Offline and Private
This application works completely on your device without needing an internet connection to run daily tasks. Your customer information, parts costs, and profits stay strictly on your local device.

1. **Bottom Navigation Bar:** Use the five navigation buttons at the bottom of the screen to quickly jump between primary daily tasks: **Dashboard**, **Billing**, **Inventory**, **Customers**, and **Reports**.

### Quick Billing Action
At the very top of the dashboard is a large **Billing** button. Tapping it opens the billing screen instantly so you can bill a customer without delay during busy shop hours.

### Date Range Controls
Choose which time period you want the dashboard cards to calculate:
- **This Month:** Shows all totals from the 1st of the current month up to the end of the month.
- **This Year:** Shows totals from January 1st through December 31st of the current year.
- **Custom Range:** Opens a calendar allowing you to choose any exact start and end date (e.g., last week or a specific weekend).

### Dashboard Statistic Cards

| Card Title | What the Number Represents | Where the Number Comes From | How to Use It |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sales** | Total money billed to customers. | Sum of all completed invoices in the selected date range. | Shows your gross garage turnover for the period. |
| **Expenses** | Total business money spent operating the shop. | Sum of all shop expenses (excluding personal expenses) plus all **paid** employee salaries in that period. | Shows what it cost you to run the business. |
| **Profit** | Net profit earned by the garage. | Calculated as: $\text{Sales} - \text{Expenses}$. | Shows true take-home business earnings before personal withdrawals. |
| **Services Completed** | Number of repair jobs finished. | Total count of customer service job cards completed during the period. | Indicates workshop workload and customer volume. |
| **Inventory Value** | Total retail value of stock on your shelves. | Sum of $(\text{Current Stock} \times \text{Selling Price})$ across all parts in your catalog. | Shows how much working capital is sitting on your shelves. |
| **Low Stock** | Count of parts that need reordering. | Number of parts where current stock is at or below the part's low-stock alert threshold. | Warning indicator telling you it is time to order stock from suppliers. |

### Low Stock Alerts Section
Located below the cards, this section lists every spare part that is running out, displaying the **Part Name**, **Part Number**, and **Available Stock vs. Reorder Level** (e.g., `2 / 5`).

### Top Selling Parts Section
Displays a ranked leaderboard of the parts most frequently installed in customer vehicles during the selected time period, showing units sold and total revenue generated.

### Step-by-Step: Creating a Customer Bill

1. **Open Billing:** Tap **Billing** in the bottom navigation bar or tap the **Billing** button on the Dashboard.
2. **Choose the Customer:**
   - **Existing Customer:** Tap **Select Customer**, scroll or search the list, and tap their name.
   - **New Customer:** Tap **New Customer**, enter their **Name**, **Mobile Number**, and **Vehicle Details** (e.g., `Royal Enfield Classic 350`), then tap **Create**. They are selected automatically.
3. **Enter the Job / Problem Description (Optional):** Type a brief summary of the repair work done (e.g., `Brake pad replacement and chain lubrication`).
4. **Enter Labour / Service Charge:** Type your service or labour fees in rupees (e.g., `350.00`). If no labour is being charged, leave it as `0`.
5. **Add Spare Parts Used:**
   - Tap **+ Add Part**.
   - A list of all parts in your catalog appears showing their current shelf stock and selling price.
   - Tap the part you installed. *(Note: Parts with 0 stock cannot be selected).*
   - Adjust the quantity using the **+** and **-** buttons. You cannot increase the quantity beyond what is physically available in your inventory.
   - Repeat for any other parts used on the vehicle.
6. **Review the Cost Breakdown:** Check the subtotal for parts, the labour charge, and the final grand total.
7. **Generate the Invoice:**
   - Tap **Generate Invoice**.
   - A confirmation dialog appears showing the customer name and cost breakdown.
   - Tap **Generate**.
8. **Automatic Updates:**
   - The application automatically generates the next sequential invoice number (e.g., `Invoice #104`).
   - The used parts are **automatically deducted from your inventory immediately**. You do not need to adjust stock manually.
   - The completed bill opens in the **Invoice Overview Screen**.

### Searching Parts
Type any part of the **Part Name**, **Part Number (SKU)**, or **Category** in the top search box. The list filters instantly. Tap the **Clear (✕)** button to restore the full list.

### Low Stock Visual Alerts
If a part's current shelf stock is at or below its low-stock alert threshold, the item is flagged with a prominent amber alert icon (⚠️), alerting you that it is time to order more.

### Adding a New Part
1. Tap the **+** button at the bottom-right of the screen.
2. Fill in the part details:
   - **Part Number / SKU:** A unique code for the part (e.g., `OIL-10W40-1L` or `PLUG-BKR6E`). *Every part must have a distinct part number.*
   - **Part Name:** Descriptive title of the component (e.g., `Synthetic Engine Oil 10W-40`).
   - **Category (Optional):** Grouping category (e.g., `Lubricants`, `Brakes`, `Electrical`, `Suspension`).
   - **Purchase Price (₹):** What you pay your supplier per unit (e.g., `280.00`).
   - **Selling Price (₹):** The price you charge customers on invoices (e.g., `380.00`).
   - **Current Stock:** The physical count currently on your shelf (e.g., `12`).
   - **Low Stock Threshold:** The alert level (default is `5`). When stock reaches this number, warning badges appear.
3. Tap **Save Part**.

### Editing an Existing Part
1. In the parts list, tap on any part card.
2. The form opens pre-filled with the current information.
3. Update the prices, threshold, or name.
4. Tap **Save Part**.

## 8. Recording Purchases (Stock-In)

When a spare parts delivery arrives from a distributor, record the delivery using **Record Purchase (Stock-In)** to update your inventory.

```text

├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤

│                                                        │
│ Quantity                                               │
│ [ 20                                                 ] │
│                                                        │

│ [ ₹ 115.00                                           ] │
│                                                        │
│ [  📦 Record Purchase                                ] │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Step-by-Step: Recording Incoming Stock
1. Tap **☰** in the top-left to open the sidebar.
2. Select **Record Purchase (Stock-In)**.
3. **Select Part:** Choose the delivered item from the dropdown menu.
4. **Quantity:** Enter the number of units received (e.g., `20`). Must be a whole number greater than 0.
5. **Purchase Price:** The form automatically fills with your last recorded purchase price. If your supplier changed their price (e.g., it rose to ₹120.00), update it here.
6. Tap **Record Purchase**.

### What Happens Automatically
- The incoming quantity is **added immediately to your current shelf stock** (e.g., if you had 4 spark plugs and purchased 20, your inventory now shows 24).
- The part's master catalog purchase price is updated to reflect the new cost.
- A permanent purchase record is logged for your financial and cost-of-goods reports.

### Recording an Expense
1. Open the sidebar (☰) and tap **Expenses**.
2. Tap the **+** button at the bottom-right.
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Category:** Select from `Rent`, `Electricity`, `Internet`, `Fuel`, `Equipment`, `Tools`, `Tea & Snacks`, or `Miscellaneous`.

## 17. Data Entry Guidelines

Accurate numbers make your financial reports trustworthy. Follow these simple tips:

- **Customer Mobile Numbers:** Enter standard 10-digit mobile numbers without special symbols or spaces (e.g., `9876543210`). This ensures the **Share on WhatsApp** button opens directly to the customer without prompting you to fix the number.
- **Part Numbers (SKUs):** Use consistent, short codes (e.g., `OIL-4T-1L`, `BRK-PAD-F`). Do not create duplicate part numbers for the same component.
- **Purchase Price vs. Selling Price:**
  - *Purchase Price:* What you paid the distributor.
  - *Selling Price:* What you charge the customer.  
  *Never set selling price lower than purchase price.*
- **Physical Stock Verification:** Perform a quick physical count of parts on your shelves once a month to ensure physical boxes match the numbers in the app.
- **Labour Charges:** If you did a free checkup, enter `0`. Never enter negative numbers.

## 18. Common Problems & Solutions

| What You See on Screen | Why It Happened | What to Do |
|---|---|---|
| **"Insufficient stock for part: ..."** | You tried to bill more units than you have on your shelf. | Either reduce the billed quantity or record a stock-in purchase before generating the invoice. |
| **"Part number already exists: ..."** | You tried to add a new part with a code already used by another part. | Use a different unique part number, or edit the existing part instead. |
| **"Please enter a valid amount (> 0)"** | An amount was left empty, typed as zero, or entered as a negative number. | Enter a positive rupee amount. |
| **"Select or create a customer first"** | You tapped Generate Invoice before choosing a customer. | Tap **Select Customer** or **New Customer** at the top of the billing screen. |
| **"Add at least one part or labour charge"** | The bill has 0 parts and 0 labour charges. | Add at least one replacement part or enter a labour charge greater than ₹0. |
| **Cannot delete a customer** | The customer has existing invoices linked to them. | Customers with service history cannot be deleted to protect accounting integrity. You can edit their contact details instead. |
| **WhatsApp did not open** | The customer's mobile number is incomplete or WhatsApp is not installed on the device. | Verify that WhatsApp is installed and that the customer's phone number is a valid 10-digit mobile number. |

## 19. Important Business Rules

1. **Billing Automatically Deducts Stock:** Whenever you complete an invoice, parts are deducted from inventory instantly. Never manually decrease stock after billing a customer.
2. **Historical Invoices Never Change:** When an invoice is created, the selling price of parts is frozen permanently on that invoice. If you increase part prices next month, your past customer receipts and past sales reports remain accurate and unchanged.
3. **Atomic Billing Safety:** If an invoice cannot be generated (for example, if a part ran out of stock halfway through), **no partial bill is saved and zero parts are deducted**. The system protects your data from corrupt states.
4. **Business Expenses vs. Personal Finance:**
   - Workshop overhead (rent, electricity, tools) belongs in **Expenses**.
   - Personal money (home groceries, family withdrawals, pocket cash) belongs in **Personal Finance**.
   - Keeping them separate guarantees that your **Net Profit** on the Dashboard reflects genuine garage earnings.

## 22. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### Q: Does this app require internet to create bills or record stock?
**A:** No. The entire system works completely offline. You only need an internet connection if you choose to share an invoice or backup file via WhatsApp or email.

#### Q: Why did my stock decrease after generating a bill?
**A:** This is normal and intentional. The application automatically reduces stock for any replacement parts added to an invoice so your inventory counts always match what is physically on your shelves.

#### Q: How do I add new stock when a delivery arrives?
**A:** Open the sidebar (☰), tap **Record Purchase (Stock-In)**, pick the delivered item, type the quantity received, and tap **Record Purchase**. Your stock count will increase immediately.

#### Q: Can I change a customer's phone number or vehicle?
**A:** Yes. Go to **Customers**, tap the **⋮** menu on the customer's card, choose **Edit**, update the details, and tap **Save Customer**.

#### Q: Can I see what was done on a customer's car six months ago?
**A:** Yes. Go to **Customers**, tap their name, and scroll through their **Service / Billing History**. Tap any past visit to see the exact parts used, labour charged, and bill amount.

#### Q: What happens if an employee arrives after 10:30 AM?
**A:** When you mark their reporting time as 10:31 AM or later, the system marks their attendance as **Late**. The employee's monthly punctuality score will reflect this automatically.

#### Q: What if I lose my phone?
**A:** If you have been creating regular backups and sending them to your personal WhatsApp or Google Drive, install the app on your new phone, go to **Backup & Restore**, tap **Restore**, and paste your backup text. All your customers, parts, and invoices will be restored immediately.
